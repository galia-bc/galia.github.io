Your GGC Phone

A shared home for Bank, Chats, games and Camera, with a lock screen, wallpaper and notifications. Phone access and some apps remain limited to eligible testers or issued handsets.

Camera

Rear and selfie views, group photos, zoom and drag-to-frame controls. One tap restores the current focus and default zoom.

Messages

Text and photo conversations, friend portraits and lock-screen previews. Eligible senders can reach GGC friends in other rooms.

Calls

Private calls between GGC friends, with speaker and hang-up controls. Call initiation is currently restricted to Galia.

Closer room interactions

Place yourself beside, in front of or behind another player, join a small group, and step away again. Group-aware camera framing follows the people together, with native pose and interaction permissions respected.

Skirmishes

Accepted keyboard duels bring an animated tug-of-war arena to room play, including supported item and theft interactions. Keyboard-layout support, native item checks and opt-out settings remain in place; initiation restrictions still apply.

Install

FUSAM: GGC is now available in the Fantastic Ultimate Solution to Addon Management catalogue. Install FUSAM, enable Galia's Gambling Club in its addon list, and let the manager load it for you. FUSAM installation instructions.

Tampermonkey / Violentmonkey: a persistent alternative. Create a userscript using the Stable or Beta template in the detailed guide, save it, then GGC loads automatically whenever you open Bondage Club. Enable GGC through one method at a time.

Bookmarklet: a quick temporary option. Save one of the bookmarklet lines as a browser bookmark, open Bondage Club, then select the bookmark. Run it again after reloading the page.

  1. Choose Stable for everyday play, or Beta for the newest features.
  2. Enable GGC in FUSAM, use an addon loader, or copy a template from the detailed guide.
  3. Reload Bondage Club, join a room, and open a character dialog.
  4. Look for the GGC radial menu.
